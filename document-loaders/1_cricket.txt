Rising with the dawn’s first light,
A cricket field shines fresh and bright.
The willow sings, the leather flies,
Beneath the endless, hopeful skies.

The bowler charges, fierce and fast,
Each delivery a spell that’s cast.
The batter waits with steady grace,
Determined in that fleeting space.

A cover drive, so smooth and clean,
A stroke of art, a timeless scene.
The crowd erupts, their voices soar,
As boundaries race from shore to shore.

The spinner weaves a clever snare,
The ball dances through the evening air.
A catch held high with fearless hands,
Changes the fate of teams and plans.

Victory blooms through grit and cheer,
Defeat still teaches year by year.
For cricket’s heart is more than fame—
It’s courage, passion, and the game.

So when the final wicket falls,
And silence gently fills the halls,
The spirit lingers, proud and true,
In every dream the game renews.
